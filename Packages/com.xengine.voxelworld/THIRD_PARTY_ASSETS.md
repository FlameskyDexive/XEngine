# Third-party assets

This package's **code is MIT**. Node **N-art** replaced the last temporary Voxel Play 2
texture (`white64.png`) with an XEngine-authored 64×64 white PNG. The asset GUID
`fdc9f888-96fc-048b-f860-0563a62b9e7e` is unchanged so `DefaultVoxel` face refs stay valid.

Grass, dirt, stone, leaves, sand, snow, water, wood, and cloud faces are owned 16×16 procedural tiles packed into the demo `Texture2DArray`.
Click SFX is generated at runtime (`VoxelSfx.CreateClickWav`).
